Custodexa upgrade preview (nothing has been changed yet)

  Installed          1.13.0
  Upgrade to         1.13.2
  Deployment         /opt/custodexa
  Master key mode    entered in the browser

What will happen
  1. Wait until all audit records are in the database, then stop the
     services (the database keeps running)
  2. Full backup: database, recordings, audit files, settings,
     certificates. About 18.4 GB; 211 GB free where backups go
  3. Switch to 1.13.2 and start it
  4. Check the version, that it is the same data as before, and that
     the keys did not change

Good to know
  - Expect 15 to 30 minutes of downtime. Nobody can connect meanwhile,
    and open connections will be cut (2 are open now)
  - This version changes the database structure once. Rolling back
    afterwards means restoring this backup; anything recorded after
    the backup is lost
  - After the upgrade the system stays sealed until someone enters
    the master key on the unseal page
  - Image checksums, signatures and build provenance are verified
  - Have someone familiar with operating this host run it inside a
    terminal tool that keeps the session alive (such as tmux or
    screen), to reduce the risk that a dropped SSH connection stops
    the upgrade
